## Hardware Definitions

This document describes the hardware decisions and choices for the Dormin Keyboard.

## Must Have

- Usage of "Panelized Non-Reversible" PCB (costs saving and easy routing).
- Seeed Studio XIAO nRF52840 Plus (20 GPIO).
- Optional components: OLED SSD1306 or Nice!View ; EC12 Rotatory Encoder.

## WIP

- RGB LEDs for status indicador ([WIP](./led_status_system.md)).
- Add tiny magnets on the side to "lock" the halves and use "unibody-like" (alternative to the pogo-pins).
- Add tiny magnets on the bottom to secure halves during transport and easy travel.
